## Direction contract

THESIS: Hungry Pathing is the works canteen at a timber mill: meals and water are timed to the shift whistle, a slate board says when to leave so nobody works hungry, and an enamel sign points to the nearest canteen instead of the fancy one across town. After the evening whistle, the town's own habits take over. It refuses the borrowed Persistent Work Areas look the site wears (teal-and-gold palette, dead demo CSS, eyebrows, pill chips, identical numbered cards, radial glow).

OWN-WORLD: A works yard at the end of a shift. Enamel signs: cream vitreous enamel (#efe8d6) with cobalt borders and lettering (#1d4a86), a chip or two of dark iron at the edges; the slate board: blue-black slate (#23292d) with chalk (#ece9e1) and chalk colours for the two needs, hunger coral (#e8735c, the mod's own logo colour) and water blue (#7cc3e6); the yard ground: warm sawdust-grey (#e3ddd0) by day, sooty timber-shed dark (#191b1d) by night. Big Shoulders (display, self-hosted, OFL) for sign lettering and headings; body system-ui; log lines in mono. The board's chalk texture and the enamel's surface are produced rasters; no CSS bevels.

STORY: One look: working beavers eat and drink before hunger hits, at the closest storage, timed to their shift; off duty nothing changes; they don't eat more; co-op safe; one file to tune. Then: the shift on the board (a working day with the buffer marked and the moment to leave), the five rules as enamel signs, the measured before/after, what never changes, co-op and the settings file, what is tested and what isn't, install.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: "Hungry Pathing" (display), the pitch "Fed before the whistle, not after.", a two-sentence lead, three facts (working hours only, evenings untouched; eats at the closest stocked storage; same amount eaten, only the timing moves), Download + Install guide, short status note. Right: the slate board, one working shift drawn as a timeline with the Hunger line falling, the three-hour buffer shaded, the moment the beaver leaves marked, the walk to the closest storage and the arrival with time in hand; beside it, a quieter chalk line showing the game's own timing running into the penalty. A control lets the visitor lengthen the walk and watch the leave time move earlier. Labelled as an illustration.

FORM: Works Canteen, candidate 6 of 7 (seed 74e1d760). Challengers weighed: rail split-flap board (competitive: clearer on timing, weaker audience fit; kept its discipline: one board holds every time in one gaze, in clock time), suminagashi, industrial streetwear, Versailles, illuminated initial (declined). Raises taken: times are always given as shift clock times or hours, never vague (split-flap); every sign has one job and one fixed layout (streetwear tag discipline, without its clothes); the board's control changes a computed result, not a canned one. Signature interaction: lengthen the walk on the slate board and the leave time moves (motion off under reduced motion: the chalk marks jump).
